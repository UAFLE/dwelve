2. Scope of Policy

This policy covers all video games, VR simulators, downloadable add-ons, in-app microtransactions, recurring subscription passes, and player support channels published by Dwelve.

3. In-App Purchases (IAP), Add-ons & Subscriptions (What, When & Why)

All financial payments are handled exclusively through Meta Quest platform gateways. Dwelve never collects, handles, or stores sensitive financial details such as credit card credentials, billing addresses, or bank authentications. We process non-financial entitlement tokens as detailed below:

Item Category & Examples What Data Is Handled When It Is Processed Why It Is Needed

4. Information We Collect

A. Provided by Meta

  • Meta User ID, username/display name, and avatar configuration data.
  • Hardware device model, firmware/OS build, region/locale code.
  • Multiplayer room session tokens and matchmaking ping.

B. Gameplay, VR Movement & Technical Telemetry

  • Vehicle physics friction telemetry, mission career saves, and lap times.
  • VR Movement & Tracking: Headset and controller spatial coordinates are used strictly in real-time on your local device to render the virtual cockpit and hands. We do not use movement data to identify individuals or for behavioral surveillance.
  • Frame latency benchmarks, error diagnostics, and crash logs.

5. How We Use Information

  • Operate and optimize our vehicle physics engines and 72/90/120 FPS render loops.
  • Authenticate ownership rights and synchronize cloud save profiles.
  • Mitigate telemetry tampering, leaderboard cheating, and griefing.

6. Analytics & Service Providers

We work exclusively with core platform providers (Meta Horizon Platform Services) and crash analytics SDKs. Dwelve does not sell, rent, or trade Meta user data or telemetry to third-party data brokers or behavioral advertisers.

7. Children's Privacy

Our titles comply with Meta account age standards. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the platform's minimum age. For inquiries, email dwelve0@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Users may request the complete deletion of their account records and progression data by emailing dwelve0@gmail.com with their Meta Display Name and Meta User ID.

9. Meta Quest Platform Precedence

Our software operates within the Meta Quest runtime ecosystem. Your device hardware usage and account settings remain bound by Meta's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
